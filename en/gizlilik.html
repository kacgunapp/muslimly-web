Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Muslimly: Prayer Times & Quran app for iOS (“Muslimly”) uses and how. In short: Muslimly has no server and does not collect personal data from you. To work, some features send requests to the third-party services listed one by one below.

No account

To use Muslimly you don't need to create an account, sign in or give information such as an email address or phone number. The app has no server.

No ads; crash reports and anonymous usage measurement

Muslimly shows no ads, does not track you across apps and websites, and does not access the advertising identifier.

Starting with version 1.0.1, to keep the app running reliably, crash and performance reports (Sentry) and anonymous usage measurement of which screens are used (PostHog, EU servers) are collected. This data is not linked to your identity; no account information, location, prayer or Qur'an progress, or anything you have written is sent; only the app version, device model, operating system version, error details and screen names are sent. This collection is turned off in development builds.

In the “App Privacy” section on the App Store, this data is declared as Crash Data, Performance Data and Product Interaction, under the heading “Data Not Linked to You”.

Your data stays on your device

The name you optionally enter, the city you choose, your settings, your prayer and task progress, your Qur'an reading progress and your bookmarks are stored only on your device. If enabled, this data is also included in your iPhone's iCloud backup; that backup belongs to your Apple account and cannot be read by Muslimly. If you turn on the Home Screen widget, a short summary (next prayer time, the day's tasks) is also written to a shared area on your device so the widget can show it.

The profile you choose at first launch (child, adult man or adult woman; if a child, girl or boy) and your school (madhhab) are also stored only on your device; they are used for the prayer instructions, the figure and the Asr time calculation, and are sent nowhere.

When you delete the app, all Muslimly data on the device is deleted too.

Sync with iCloud (optional)

If you turn on the “Sync with iCloud” option in Settings (it is off by default), your progress and settings are written to your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value store and synced with your other devices on the same account. This data stays in your Apple account; since Muslimly has no server, it does not reach us and we cannot see it. This service is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Special days mode information, purchase status, widget permission and the app icon choice are not written to iCloud; they stay only on your device. When you turn the option off, syncing stops.

Special days mode

Special days mode, which can be turned on in the woman and girl profiles, and the information on which days it was on are kept only in the app's own storage on your device. This information is not sent to any server; it is not written to the shared area read by the Home Screen widget, does not appear in notification texts and is not recorded (no logs are kept). The widget and notifications are the same whether special days mode is on or off. When you turn it off, past days stay on the device for at most 90 days, only for the streak calculation; when you delete the app, this information is deleted too. If enabled, it is included in your iPhone's iCloud backup together with your other app data.

Location

If you grant location permission, your location is used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. If you don't grant location permission, you can choose your city from the list.

When you open the “Nearby mosques” screen, your location, rounded to about 1 km (two decimal places), is sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service to find nearby mosques. As with every internet request, your IP address also reaches this service; apart from that, no information identifying you is sent. The city found by location is also stored on the device with the same precision, rounded to about 1 km. Mosque data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). If the Overpass service cannot be reached, the search is done with Apple Maps on your device; in that case your location, rounded in the same way to two decimal places (about 1 km), goes to Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy. Muslimly does not store this location; the list of mosques found is kept only in your device's memory for at most 30 minutes.

Maps and directions (Apple Maps)

The maps on the “Nearby mosques” and “Kaaba” screens are shown with Apple Maps. Map images for the region shown on screen are loaded from Apple; if you have granted location permission, your location is also shown on the map. This service is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

When you tap a mosque's “Directions” button, Apple Maps opens with the mosque's name and coordinates; the route is calculated in Apple Maps. This happens only when you tap.

Camera

If you open the “Qibla camera” screen, the camera image is shown only on screen, behind the Qibla arrow. The image is not saved, not processed and does not leave your device. If you don't grant camera permission, you can still find the Qibla with the compass and the map.

Qur'an and supplication audio

The audio comes inside the app; no request is sent to any service to play it. Verse audio that earlier versions downloaded from the internet to the device is deleted from the device at the first launch after the app is updated.

Read aloud

Reading Turkish texts aloud and the Arabic read-aloud that can be turned on in Settings (synthetic voice; not a recitation; off by default) are done on your device with your iPhone's own speech voices. The text being read is not sent to any service.

Sharing

When you tap the “Share my progress” button, your progress summary (streak, goal, prayer and dhikr counts) opens in the iOS share menu. The text goes only to the app or person you choose; Muslimly shares nothing on its own.

Purchases

This version of Muslimly has no purchases; the service below is not active and sends no requests.

If an optional Premium subscription is offered in the future: purchases and payment are processed by Apple; Muslimly does not see your payment information. The RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) service is used to verify whether your subscription is valid. This service receives a randomly generated user number that is not linked to your name or Apple Account, your purchase history (product, date, price, currency, country) and the versions of the app and of iOS. This information is not used for advertising or tracking and is not shared with third parties for that purpose. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings; refund requests are made through Apple.

Notifications

Prayer time notifications and reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Remote (push) notifications are not used; no registration with any server is made for notifications.

Widget, Live Activity, Siri and Focus

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the prayer time countdown on the Lock Screen (Live Activity), Siri shortcuts and the Focus filter run on your device; they read the prayer time information from the app's shared area on the device. No request is sent to any server for these.

Children

Muslimly has a child mode. Since the app does not collect personal data from any user, including children, there is also no special data processing for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page with a new effective date.
